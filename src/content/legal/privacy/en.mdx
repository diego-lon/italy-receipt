This policy explains what personal data ItalyReceipt (italyreceipt.com) processes, why, and what choices you have. In short: the receipt generator works entirely in your browser and we never receive what you type into it. Cookies for analytics and personalised advertising are used only if you agree.

## Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Diego Longo, who runs ItalyReceipt as a personal project. You can reach him at [dev.diego.longo@gmail.com](mailto:dev.diego.longo@gmail.com) for any question about this policy or your data.

## The receipt generator: your data stays on your device

The generator runs entirely in your browser:

- What you type into the form, such as names, codice fiscale, addresses, amounts, IBAN and notes, is **never sent** to us or to any server.
- To pre-fill the form on your next visit, it is saved only in your browser's local storage, under the key `italyreceipt:draft:v1`. It stays on your device until you delete it.
- You can delete it at any time with the **Clear saved data** button in the generator, or by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.
- PDFs are generated locally on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.

Because we never receive this data, we can't see, change or delete it for you: it is entirely under your control. If you use a shared computer, clear the saved data when you have finished.

## Cookies and local storage

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `italyreceipt:draft:v1` (local storage) | ItalyReceipt | Saves the generator form on your device | Until you delete it |
| `italyreceipt:consent:v1` (local storage) | ItalyReceipt | Remembers your cookie choices | 6 months |
| `_ga`, `_ga_<ID>` | Google Analytics | Distinguish visitors and sessions for statistics | Up to 2 years, only with consent |
| `__gads`, `__gpi`, `IDE` | Google AdSense | Advertising, including personalised ads, and ad measurement | Per Google, up to 13 months in the EEA, only with consent |

The two ItalyReceipt entries are used only by the site's own code in your browser and are never sent to us.

## Analytics: Google Analytics 4

We use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Ltd, to understand how many people visit the site, which pages are useful and how the generator is used, through aggregated statistics.

- Analytics cookies are set **only after you consent** to analytics.
- According to Google, Google Analytics 4 does not log or store the IP addresses of users in the EU.
- Data is kept according to our Google Analytics settings, for up to 14 months.

To learn more, see [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Advertising: Google AdSense

To keep ItalyReceipt free, the site shows ads served by Google AdSense, provided by Google Ireland Ltd.

- **With your consent**, Google may use cookies to show personalised ads based on your interests and to measure ad performance.
- **Without your consent**, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, which do not use cookies for personalisation.

We use Google Consent Mode v2: before you make a choice, advertising and analytics storage are set to denied, and they change only according to what you choose. You can manage Google's ad personalisation at [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com) and read how Google uses data at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Your consent choices

On your first visit, a banner lets you **Accept all**, **Reject all** or **Customize** your choice by category: strictly necessary, analytics and advertising. Refusing is as easy as accepting, and the generator works in exactly the same way whatever you choose.

- Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage under `italyreceipt:consent:v1` for 6 months, after which we ask again.
- You can give, refuse or change your consent at any time with the **Cookie settings** link in the footer.
- Withdrawing consent doesn't affect processing that took place before you withdrew it.
- You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
