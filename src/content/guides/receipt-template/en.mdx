Looking for an invoice template for Italy without a VAT number? For occasional work, what you need is a receipt, the *ricevuta per prestazione occasionale*, not an invoice. This page explains every field, shows a complete sample in Italian with an English explanation, covers the variants for different clients and lists the mistakes to avoid.

<Callout type="info" title="A receipt, not an invoice">
Without a *Partita IVA* (VAT number) you can't issue a *fattura*. Occasional work is outside the scope of VAT (art. 5 DPR 633/1972) and outside electronic invoicing, so a signed paper or PDF receipt is the right document.
</Callout>

## The fields, one by one

| Field | What to write |
|---|---|
| Ricevuta n. and Data | A progressive number, for example 1/2026, and the date of issue |
| Prestatore | Your full name, address and *codice fiscale* (tax code) |
| Committente | The client's name, address and VAT number or tax code |
| Declaration | That the work was occasional, without subordination or coordination |
| Descrizione | What you did and when |
| Compenso lordo | The gross fee |
| Ritenuta | The withholding tax, or the reason there is none |
| Contributo INPS | Your one-third INPS share, only above €5,000 a year |
| Netto a pagare | The net amount the client pays you |
| VAT note | Outside the scope of VAT under art. 5 DPR 633/1972 |
| €5,000 declaration | Whether your occasional income this year is above €5,000 |
| Modalità di pagamento | Bank transfer with your IBAN, or another method |
| Marca da bollo | The €2 stamp above €77.47, with its serial number |
| Luogo e data, firma | Place, date and your signature |

A few fields need more care:

- **Description.** Be specific about the job and when you did it: "Logo design for the spring campaign, delivered on 20 October 2026" works, "consulting" doesn't.
- **Withholding.** It depends on the client and on your tax residence, as the variants below show.
- **INPS.** These lines appear only once your occasional income for the calendar year, from all clients, goes over €5,000.
- **Stamp.** Buy the €2 *marca da bollo* on or before the receipt date, stick it on the original and copy its 14-digit serial number onto the receipt.

## A complete sample receipt

Here is example A: Anna Bianchi, an Italian resident, translates a manual for an Italian company and is paid €1,000. It's her first occasional job of the year, so she is below €5,000. All codes are placeholders.

```text
RICEVUTA PER PRESTAZIONE DI LAVORO AUTONOMO OCCASIONALE

Ricevuta n. 1/2026                                  Data: 10/11/2026

Prestatore: Anna Bianchi
Via Roma 1, 20121 Milano (MI) - C.F. XXXXXX00X00X000X

Committente: Alfa S.r.l.
Corso Italia 10, 00198 Roma (RM) - P. IVA 00000000000

La sottoscritta Anna Bianchi, codice fiscale XXXXXX00X00X000X, dichiara
di aver svolto in favore di Alfa S.r.l. la seguente prestazione di
lavoro autonomo occasionale, senza vincolo di subordinazione e in
assenza di abitualità, continuità e coordinamento (art. 2222 c.c.;
art. 67, comma 1, lett. l), D.P.R. 917/1986), e di ricevere il relativo
compenso come di seguito indicato.

Descrizione: traduzione dall'inglese all'italiano di un manuale
tecnico di 20 pagine, consegnata il 5/11/2026.

Compenso lordo                                            € 1.000,00
Ritenuta d'acconto 20% (art. 25 D.P.R. 600/1973)           - € 200,00
Netto a pagare                                              € 800,00

Operazione esclusa dal campo di applicazione dell'IVA ai sensi
dell'art. 5 del D.P.R. 633/1972, per mancanza del requisito
dell'abitualità.

Il prestatore dichiara che, nell'anno solare in corso, i compensi
percepiti per prestazioni di lavoro autonomo occasionale, compreso il
presente, non superano € 5.000,00 e che pertanto non sussiste l'obbligo
contributivo alla Gestione Separata INPS (art. 44, comma 2,
D.L. 269/2003).

Imposta di bollo di € 2,00 assolta sull'originale.
Identificativo marca da bollo: 00000000000000

Modalità di pagamento: bonifico bancario
IBAN IT00 X000 0000 0000 0000 0000 000

Luogo e data: Milano, 10/11/2026

Firma del prestatore: ______________________
```

## What each part means

- **Ricevuta per prestazione di lavoro autonomo occasionale**: receipt for occasional self-employed work.
- **Prestatore** and **Committente**: you, the provider, and your client.
- **The declaration** ("La sottoscritta... dichiara"): you declare that the work was occasional, without subordination, habituality, continuity or coordination (art. 2222 of the Civil Code and art. 67(1)(l) TUIR), and that you are paid as shown below.
- **Compenso lordo, Ritenuta d'acconto, Netto a pagare**: gross fee, 20% withholding tax (an advance on your income tax), net amount payable.
- **Operazione esclusa dal campo di applicazione dell'IVA**: outside the scope of VAT, because the activity isn't habitual.
- **The €5,000 declaration** ("Il prestatore dichiara..."): your occasional income this calendar year, including this receipt, doesn't exceed €5,000, so no INPS contributions are due (art. 44, paragraph 2, DL 269/2003).
- **Imposta di bollo... assolta sull'originale**: the €2 stamp duty is paid on the original, followed by the stamp's serial number.
- **Modalità di pagamento, Luogo e data, Firma del prestatore**: payment method, place and date, your signature.

If you charge the stamp to the client, the receipt adds a "Rimborso imposta di bollo" line of €2 to the amount payable, so Anna would receive €802. The €2 isn't subject to withholding.

## Variants for other situations

### Private client

For €500 paid by a private individual, there's no withholding and the net equals the gross. The withholding line is replaced by a note:

```text
Compenso lordo                                              € 500,00
Netto a pagare                                              € 500,00

Compenso non soggetto a ritenuta d'acconto: il committente non riveste
la qualifica di sostituto d'imposta.
```

It means "no withholding tax: the client is not a withholding agent". The same applies to clients under the flat-rate regime (*regime forfettario*). At €500, the €2 stamp is still needed.

### Foreign client

```text
Compenso non soggetto a ritenuta d'acconto: committente non residente
privo di stabile organizzazione in Italia.
```

This means "no withholding tax: non-resident client without a permanent establishment in Italy". As an Italian resident, you declare the income in your tax return.

A bilingual receipt, with Italian next to English, Spanish, French or German, keeps the legal wording and is easier for foreign accountants to process.

### Above €5,000

This is example C: €3,000 already received this year, a new €4,000 receipt for an Italian company, and the standard 33.72% INPS rate.

```text
Compenso lordo                                            € 4.000,00
Ritenuta d'acconto 20% (art. 25 D.P.R. 600/1973)           - € 800,00
Contributo INPS Gestione Separata a carico del prestatore (1/3)
                                                           - € 224,80
Netto a pagare                                            € 2.975,20

Il prestatore dichiara che, nell'anno solare in corso, i compensi
percepiti per prestazioni di lavoro autonomo occasionale hanno superato
€ 5.000,00 (totale, compreso il presente: € 7.000,00). Sulla quota
eccedente di € 2.000,00 è dovuto il contributo alla Gestione Separata
INPS nella misura del 33,72% (art. 44, comma 2, D.L. 269/2003), per 1/3
a carico del prestatore (€ 224,80) e per 2/3 a carico del committente
(€ 449,60).
```

The INPS line is your one-third share. The declaration gives your total for the year, the excess, the rate and both shares; the client pays its €449.60 on top of the gross. The [€5,000 limit guide](/en/5000-euro-limit-italy/) explains the calculation and the reduced 24% rate.

### Non-resident worker

If you are not an Italian tax resident and an Italian company pays you for work done in Italy, the withholding becomes a 30% final tax:

```text
Compenso lordo                                            € 1.000,00
Ritenuta a titolo d'imposta 30% (art. 25, c. 2, D.P.R. 600/1973)
                                                           - € 300,00
Netto a pagare                                              € 700,00
```

If a double tax treaty removes the withholding, the line is replaced by this note:

```text
Ritenuta non applicata in base alla convenzione contro le doppie
imposizioni tra l'Italia e lo Stato di residenza del prestatore.
```

<Callout type="warning" title="Treaty relief needs paperwork">
The client can only apply a treaty if you provide a certificate of tax residence from your country and the relevant Agenzia delle Entrate form. Agree on this before you issue the receipt. See [prestazione occasionale for foreigners](/en/prestazione-occasionale-foreigners/).
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- **Charging VAT or calling it a fattura.** Without a Partita IVA there is no VAT on occasional work.
- **Forgetting the stamp**, or using one dated after the receipt. Above €77.47 it's required.
- **Using 15% or 5% withholding.** Those are flat-rate regime tax rates for Partita IVA holders. The correct options are 20%, none, or 30% for non-residents.
- **Leaving out the declarations.** Without the occasional-work and €5,000 statements, the client can't tell whether INPS contributions are due.
- **Applying withholding to the stamp.** The €2 reimbursement is not subject to withholding.

## FAQ

### Do I need the €5,000 declaration if I earn less than that?

Yes, include it: below €5,000 it confirms to the client that no INPS contributions are due.

### Who pays for the marca da bollo?

You buy it and stick it on the original, but you can charge the €2 to the client. That amount isn't subject to withholding.

### Can I just send a PDF?

Email the PDF so the client can pay you. If the receipt needs the €2 stamp, the stamp goes on the signed paper original, so deliver or post that too and keep a copy.

### Can I use the same template for every client?

Yes, but change the parts that depend on the client and on you: the withholding line or its note, the INPS lines and the €5,000 declaration. The generator switches this wording automatically.
