Without a VAT number (*Partita IVA*) you can't issue a real invoice (*fattura*), but you can still get paid for occasional work. The document you need is a receipt for occasional self-employed work, the *ricevuta per prestazione occasionale*. Once you know what goes on it, it takes a few minutes. This guide walks you through it step by step, with worked examples for the most common cases.

## Before you start

Have these ready:

- **Your *codice fiscale*** (Italian tax code). Foreigners can get one from the Agenzia delle Entrate or an Italian consulate.
- **Your client's details**: name or company name, address, and tax code or VAT number.
- **Who the client is.** An Italian business, entity or professional with a VAT number usually withholds tax; a private individual or a foreign client doesn't.
- **Your tax residence.** Italian residents and non-residents are taxed differently.
- **How much you've been paid this calendar year** (gross) for occasional work, from all clients. You need it for the €5,000 INPS threshold.

Make sure the job really is occasional: if you work regularly for the same client, or occasional jobs have become your ongoing activity, you need a Partita IVA. The [guide to working without a VAT number](/en/freelance-italy-without-vat/) explains the difference.

Agree with the client whether the price is gross (before withholding) or net (what you take home). Withholding is calculated on the gross, so if you agreed €800 net with an Italian company, the receipt shows €1,000 gross. The [receipt generator](/#generator) can work this out for you with its Start from the net amount option.
</Callout>

## Step 1: Number, date and place

Give each receipt a progressive number, such as 1/2026, 2/2026 and so on. A simple sequence makes every receipt easy to identify and helps you keep track of your yearly total. Then add the date of issue and the place, usually the town where you live.

## Step 2: Your details and the client's

Under *Prestatore* (provider), write your full name, address and codice fiscale. Under *Committente* (client), write the client's name or company name, address and VAT number or tax code. For a foreign client, use its foreign address and its tax ID, if it has one.

## Step 3: Describe the work

Say what you did and when, clearly enough that anyone reading the receipt understands the job, for example "Translation of a 20-page technical manual from English into Italian, delivered on 5 November 2026". Avoid vague wording such as "consulting" with no detail, and don't describe an ongoing service like monthly support, which suggests the work is habitual.

## Step 4: Calculate the amounts

The receipt starts from the **gross fee** (*compenso lordo*) and subtracts what the client has to withhold:

- **withholding tax**: 20% if you are an Italian resident and the client is an Italian withholding agent; none for private and foreign clients; 30% if you are non-resident and an Italian withholding agent pays you for work done in Italy;
- **your INPS share**: only if your occasional income this year goes over [the €5,000 threshold](/en/5000-euro-limit-italy/);
- what's left is the **net amount payable** (*netto a pagare*).

Here are the four main scenarios:

| Scenario | Gross | Withholding | Your INPS share | Net to you |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Italian company, Italian-resident worker | €1,000 | €200 (20%) | None | €800 |
| B. Private individual | €500 | None | None | €500 |
| C. Italian company, over €5,000 | €4,000 | €800 (20%) | €224.80 | €2,975.20 |
| D. Italian company, non-resident worker | €1,000 | €300 (30%) | None | €700 |

<Callout type="example" title="Example C step by step">
You have already received €3,000 this year and now bill €4,000 to an Italian company. Your total becomes €7,000, so contributions are due on the €2,000 excess. At 33.72% that's €674.40: the client pays two thirds (€449.60) and withholds your third (€224.80) from your payment. Your net is €4,000 minus €800 withholding minus €224.80, so €2,975.20, and the job costs the client €4,449.60. At the reduced 24% rate the contribution is €480 (€160 yours, €320 the client's) and your net is €3,040.
</Callout>

Receipts above €77.47 also need the €2 stamp (see Step 6). If you charge it to the client, add it to the amount payable: in example A the client pays you €802. The €2 is not subject to withholding.

## Step 5: Add the mandatory wording

A prestazione occasionale receipt carries a few standard statements, usually in Italian:

- **VAT note**: the amount is outside the scope of VAT under art. 5 DPR 633/1972, because the activity is not habitual;
- **withholding line**, for example "Ritenuta d'acconto 20% (art. 25 D.P.R. 600/1973)", or the reason none applies, such as a client that is not a withholding agent;
- **declaration of occasional work**: you did the work without subordination, habituality, continuity or coordination (art. 2222 c.c.; art. 67, paragraph 1, letter l, TUIR);
- **€5,000 declaration**: whether your occasional income this calendar year, including this receipt, is above or below €5,000, so the client knows whether INPS contributions apply;
- **payment details**: your IBAN or another payment method.

Don't add VAT and don't call the document a fattura. The [receipt template guide](/en/invoice-template-italy/) shows the full Italian wording with an English explanation of each line.

## Step 6: Apply the €2 stamp

If the gross is more than €77.47, buy a €2 *marca da bollo* at a tobacconist (*tabaccheria*), stick it on the original receipt and write its 14-digit serial number on the receipt.

<Callout type="warning" title="Check the stamp date">
The issue date printed on the stamp must be the same as, or earlier than, the date of the receipt. Buy the stamp first, then date the receipt.
</Callout>

## Step 7: Sign and send

Sign the receipt. Email the PDF to the client so they can process the payment, then hand over or post the signed original with the stamp. Keep a copy for yourself.

## What happens on the client's side

An Italian business, entity or professional acting as a withholding agent:

- pays you the net amount;
- pays the withholding to the State with an F24 form (tax code 1040) by the 16th of the following month;
- above €5,000, pays INPS both its two thirds and your third of the contributions;
- gives you a *Certificazione Unica* (CU) by 16 March of the following year.

A client that is a business (*imprenditore*) must also notify the Labour Inspectorate (INL) before the work starts. That's the client's obligation, not yours. Private individuals and foreign clients simply pay you: there's no CU, and you declare the income in your own tax return.

## Keep good records

- Keep a copy of every receipt, and proof of payment, for at least five years.
- Track what you **receive** in each calendar year. Both income tax and the €5,000 threshold work on a cash basis, so a receipt issued in December but paid in January counts towards the following year.
- When your CUs arrive, check them against your receipts: the withholding they show is credited against your income tax in the 730 or Redditi PF return.

## Why it isn't an electronic invoice

Electronic invoicing through Italy's *Sistema di Interscambio* (SDI) is for transactions within the VAT system. Prestazione occasionale is outside the scope of VAT, so your receipt is a plain paper or PDF document, not an e-invoice, and it doesn't go through the SDI. If a client's accounts team asks for an XML invoice, explain that without a Partita IVA a receipt for occasional work is the correct document.

## FAQ

### Is the amount on the receipt gross or net?

The main figure is the gross fee; withholding and any INPS share are subtracted to show the net amount payable. If you agreed a net price, work back to the gross: €800 net from an Italian company means €1,000 gross.

### My client uses the flat-rate regime. Do they withhold?

No. Clients under the *regime forfettario* are not required to withhold, so they normally pay you the gross and you declare it yourself. In the generator, choose the Private individual client type.

### Do I need to send a paper original?

If the receipt needs the €2 stamp, yes: the stamp goes on the original. Email the PDF first, then deliver or post the signed original and keep a copy.

### What if I don't know whether my client withholds tax?

Ask before you issue the receipt. Companies, associations, other entities, and businesses or professionals with a VAT number (except flat-rate ones) withhold 20%; private individuals and foreign clients don't. If you are not an Italian tax resident, read [prestazione occasionale for foreigners](/en/prestazione-occasionale-foreigners/).
