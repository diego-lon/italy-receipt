Freelance in Italy Without a VAT Number

If you're an expat living in Italy, you might be wondering: Can you work legally without opening a Partita IVA?

The answer is yes — thanks to Prestazione Occasionale.

What is Prestazione Occasionale?

It is a legal way to work occasionally in Italy without registering as a freelancer. It is designed for short-term or one-off work.

The €5,000 Limit

If you earn under €5,000 per year, you don’t need to pay social security (INPS). Above that, additional rules apply.

How to Invoice Without VAT

You must issue a Prestazione Occasionale receipt, including:

  • Your personal details
  • Client details
  • Gross amount
  • Withholding tax
  • Legal reference (Art. 5 DPR 633/72)

Taxes for Expats

Italian residents usually pay 20% withholding tax, while non-residents may pay 30%.

When You Need a VAT Number

If your work becomes continuous or regular, you must open a Partita IVA.
