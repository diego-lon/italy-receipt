The €5,000 Freelance Limit in Italy

The €5,000 threshold is one of the most important rules for freelancers in Italy.

What is the €5,000 Limit?

It is the maximum yearly income you can earn from occasional work without paying INPS contributions.

What Happens If You Exceed It?

If you go above €5,000, you must register with INPS and pay social security contributions.

Example

If you earn €6,000, contributions apply only to the amount above €5,000.
