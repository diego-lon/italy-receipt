Prestazione Occasionale for Foreigners

If you're a foreigner living in Italy, Prestazione Occasionale allows you to work legally without opening a VAT number.

Who Can Use It?

Expats, digital nomads, and residents doing occasional work.

Tax Rules

Non-residents typically pay a 30% withholding tax.

Common Mistakes

  • Working regularly for one client
  • Exceeding €5,000 without registering
